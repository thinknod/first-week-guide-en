# First-Week Interview Questions

**Goal:** Understand what new hires actually did and felt during their first week.
**Time:** 30 minutes per person (about 2–3 minutes per question, plus a few minutes to open and close)

**Tips for the interviewer**
- Ask about one specific moment. If the answer drifts into "usually…" or "in general…", bring it back: "Think of that one time. What happened next?"
- Follow up with "What did you do then?" or "How did you feel at that moment?" Don't offer your own ideas.
- Stay neutral. Don't react to answers as good or bad.

---

1. Think back to your very first morning. Walk me through what happened, step by step, from the moment you arrived or logged in until lunch.

2. Tell me about the last time in your first week when you got stuck and didn't know what to do next. What were you trying to do, and what did you do then?

3. Think of the last time you needed to ask someone a question that week. Who did you ask, how did you reach them, and what was going through your mind just before you asked?

4. Tell me about the last time you had to find something on your own, such as a document, a tool, or a rule. Where did you look first, and what happened after that?

5. Describe the moment in your first week when you felt most unsure or uneasy. What was happening right then, and what did you do next?

6. Walk me through the last time you set up a tool or got access to an account. What were the steps, and what happened when something didn't go as expected?

7. Tell me about the last meeting that week where you didn't fully follow what people were discussing. What did you do during the meeting, and what did you do after it?

8. Think about when you first felt you had done something useful for the team. What were you working on, who was involved, and how did you feel?

9. Pick one evening at the end of a day that week. What did you do right after work, and what was on your mind about the day?

10. Think of the last time your manager or onboarding buddy gave you instructions that week. How did the instructions reach you, and what did you do with them afterward?
