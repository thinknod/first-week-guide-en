1. Questions from this week
- Where do I change or reset my groupware password?
  Groupware home > My info > Password. If you forgot it, use "Forgot password" on the login screen. (Wiki: IT > Password, contact: IT Support)
- What do I do if my computer can't find the printer?
  Each floor has a different printer name. Tell IT Support the name on the label next to the printer. (Wiki: IT > Printer, contact: IT Support)
- If I'm going to be late, who do I tell?
  Post in the team chat first and give the reason. (Wiki: HR > Attendance, contact: HR)
- Where do I apply for business cards?
  Submit the Business Support request form. Include the English spelling of your name. (Wiki: General Affairs > Business cards, contact: Business Support)
- Is there a deadline for travel expenses?
  Yes, within one week after the trip. File an expense claim with photos of the receipts. (Wiki: General Affairs > Travel expenses, contact: Business Support)
