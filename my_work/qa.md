## 2. Grouped questions and answers

| Topic | From # | Question (email wording) | Answer | Wiki | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate card | 1, 9 | Who do I apply to for a corporate card? | Get your team lead's approval, then submit the Business Support request form. Usually three days. | General Affairs > Corporate card | Business Support |
| Meeting rooms | 2, 10 | How do I book a meeting room? | Groupware > Resource booking > Meeting rooms. 30-minute slots. | General Affairs > Meeting rooms | Business Support |
| Leave | 3 | Who approves leave requests? | Your team lead. Apply in Groupware > Attendance > Leave request. You can use leave from the month you join. | HR > Leave | HR |
| Travel expenses | 4 | Where do I upload travel expense receipts? | File an expense claim with photos of the receipts within one week after the trip. | General Affairs > Travel expenses | Business Support |
| Password | 5 | Where do I change my groupware password? | Groupware home > My info > Password. If you forgot it, use "Forgot password" on the login screen. | IT > Password | IT Support |
| Email signature | 6 | Is there a team template for the email signature? | Yes, your buddy has it. Add it in Mail > Settings > Signature. | IT > Mail | Your buddy |
| Printer | 7 | What do I do if my computer can't find the printer? | Each floor has a different printer name. Tell IT Support the name on the label next to the printer. | IT > Printer | IT Support |
| Attendance | 8 | Where do I clock in? | Groupware > Attendance > Clock in. | HR > Attendance | HR |
| Required training | 11 | When do I have to finish the required training? | Two courses within your first month. Sign up in the training system. | HR > Required training | HR |
