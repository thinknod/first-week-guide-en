Subject: [First week] Questions from this week and what to prepare for next week (Oct 24)

Hi, this is your onboarding coordinator. Thank you for a good week.

1. Questions from this week
- Who do I apply to for a corporate card?
  Get your team lead's approval, then submit the Business Support request form. Usually three days. (Wiki: General Affairs > Corporate card, contact: Business Support)
- How do I book a meeting room?
  Groupware > Resource booking > Meeting rooms. 30-minute slots. (Wiki: General Affairs > Meeting rooms, contact: Business Support)
- Who approves leave requests?
  Your team lead. Apply in Groupware > Attendance > Leave request. You can use leave from the month you join. (Wiki: HR > Leave, contact: HR)
- Where do I upload travel expense receipts?
  File an expense claim with photos of the receipts within one week after the trip. (Wiki: General Affairs > Travel expenses, contact: Business Support)
- Where do I change my groupware password?
  Groupware home > My info > Password. If you forgot it, use "Forgot password" on the login screen. (Wiki: IT > Password, contact: IT Support)
- Is there a team template for the email signature?
  Yes, your buddy has it. Add it in Mail > Settings > Signature. (Wiki: IT > Mail, contact: your buddy)
- What do I do if my computer can't find the printer?
  Each floor has a different printer name. Tell IT Support the name on the label next to the printer. (Wiki: IT > Printer, contact: IT Support)
- Where do I clock in?
  Groupware > Attendance > Clock in. (Wiki: HR > Attendance, contact: HR)
- When do I have to finish the required training?
  Two courses within your first month. Sign up in the training system. (Wiki: HR > Required training, contact: HR)

2. For next week
