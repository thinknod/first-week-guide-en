# 05 User test: First Week Guide

Fictional example. Testers and results are made up for the course.

## Three tasks (given on cards, not explained out loud)

1. Imagine it is your second day. Mark one of today's tasks as done.
2. Find out who you apply to for a corporate card.
3. Find out who to ask when the printer does not work.

## Results (5 people)

| Tester | Task 1 | Task 2 | Task 3 | What we saw |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Done, 12 s | Done, 9 s | Done, 15 s | Tapped the filter box first |
| 2 | Done, 20 s | Done, 6 s | Done, 8 s | Did not see the Day 2 button at first |
| 3 | Failed | Done, 11 s | Done, 10 s | Tapped "Done" twice and cleared the mark |
| 4 | Done, 9 s | Done, 7 s | Done, 22 s | Searched "printing" instead of "printer" and got no answer |
| 5 | Done, 15 s | Done, 8 s | Done, 9 s | Smiled at the "Can I ask this?" card |

Success criterion (4 of 5 people within 30 seconds) was met for all three tasks.
